Add the Monark site shell: `site-header`, `site-brand`, `demo-chip`, `locale-switch` and `theme-toggle`, the standard header of Monark-branded product sites. Install `@monark/site-header` to get all five; they take `href`/`LinkComponent`/`pathname` props instead of importing a router or i18n library. `demo-chip` adds the `--primary-ink` token.
